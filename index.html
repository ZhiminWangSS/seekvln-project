01
Problem & motivation

Progress Myopia

The next step depends on evidence
the agent may not see.

A landmark can lie outside the camera’s view. An instruction can leave a turn ambiguous. Yet navigation agents may remain confident and keep moving on insufficient evidence.

We call this Progress Myopia. SeekVLN learns to acquire the observations needed to ground task progress before committing to an action.

02
Methodology

Learn to seek useful evidence.

SeekVLN chooses between navigating directly and seeking additional views. After seeking, it identifies the relevant evidence, reasons about completed and upcoming subgoals, and acts. Two training stages teach this behavior.

Stage 1 · Supervised fine-tuning

FRG Build the seeking prior.

Future-guided Reverse Generation works backward from future expert actions to enrich offline trajectories with supplementary views and evidence annotations. This provides seeking and progress-reasoning supervision without additional expert interaction.

Stage 2 · Reinforcement fine-tuning

C2PO Reward useful seeking.

Counterfactual Contrastive Policy Optimization compares seeking and direct-navigation branches from the same state. Their difference in subsequent progress assigns credit to the seeking decision.

Counterfactual branches are sampled during training only; evaluation uses a single navigation policy.

03
Results

More reliable navigation.

Both training stages improve performance over Aux-Think. On R2R-CE and RxR-CE validation-unseen, the full model improves success rate by 12.7 and 7.5 percentage points, respectively. The complete benchmark comparison is reproduced below.

FRG-SFT establishes the initial behavior; C2PO further improves when to seek and how to use the acquired evidence. Full results and evaluation protocols are available in the paper ↗.

Behavioral analysis

Does seeking more often help?

+

On a separate 100-episode R2R-CE subset, adaptive seeking reaches 73% success with a 29.8% seek rate. Periodic seeking reaches 62% success while seeking at 50% of decisions. This supports learning when evidence is needed.

During reinforcement fine-tuning, beneficial action changes increase from 45.6% to 55.9%. Mean short-horizon progress gain also improves, indicating that the acquired evidence increasingly benefits navigation.

Qualitative results

What evidence does the agent find?

+

In simulation, side views reveal a dining area and a hallway beyond a kitchen island. The physical-robot example at the top of this page shows the same principle: locate the unseen chair before turning toward it.

The physical-robot rollout is qualitative and does not establish a real-world benchmark success rate.
